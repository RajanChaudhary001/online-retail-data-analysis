
# 📊 Online Retail Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Online Retail Dataset** from the UCI Machine Learning Repository.

The goal of this project is to understand retail sales performance, identify top-performing products and countries, analyze monthly sales trends, and generate useful business insights and recommendations.

The project follows a complete Data Analytics workflow:

**Data Collection → Data Cleaning → Data Processing → KPI Analysis → Data Visualization → Business Insights → Recommendations**

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** Online Retail Dataset

**Source:** UCI Machine Learning Repository

The dataset contains transactions from a UK-based online retail company.

### Original Dataset

- Rows: 541,909
- Columns: 8
- Time Period: December 2010 – December 2011
- Countries: Multiple countries

### Main Columns

- `InvoiceNo`
- `StockCode`
- `Description`
- `Quantity`
- `InvoiceDate`
- `UnitPrice`
- `CustomerID`
- `Country`

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The following data cleaning steps were performed:

- Removed duplicate records
- Handled missing product descriptions
- Converted `InvoiceDate` into datetime format
- Removed invalid/negative quantities
- Removed zero/negative unit prices
- Created a new `Revenue` column
- Calculated business KPIs

### Revenue Calculation

```text
Revenue = Quantity × UnitPrice

💡 Key Business Insights

🇬🇧 1. United Kingdom is the dominant market

The United Kingdom generated approximately £9.90M in revenue, making it the strongest revenue-generating market.

Business impact:
The business has a strong dependency on the UK market.

Recommendation:
Maintain the UK customer base while gradually developing other international markets.


---

📅 2. Revenue varies significantly by month

November recorded approximately £1.50M in revenue and was one of the strongest months.

Business impact:
Demand can vary across different periods.

Recommendation:
Plan inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of high-demand periods.


---

📦 3. DOTCOM POSTAGE is among the highest-revenue items

DOTCOM POSTAGE generated approximately £206K in recorded revenue.

Business impact:
Postage-related transactions can have a significant effect on revenue analysis.

Recommendation:
Analyze product revenue separately from postage/service-related revenue.


---

🌍 4. Broad product and geographic coverage

The dataset contains approximately 3,922 products across 38 countries.

Business impact:
This provides opportunities for product and market segmentation.

Recommendation:
Identify high-performing products and markets and optimize lower-performing areas.


---

⚠️ Data Quality Consideration

One important data-quality issue is missing CustomerID information.

The original dataset contains approximately:

135,080 missing CustomerID records

This can affect customer-level analysis such as:

Customer segmentation

Repeat purchase analysis

Customer retention

Customer lifetime value


For overall sales analysis, these transactions were retained.

For customer-specific analysis, records with missing CustomerID should be handled separately.


---

🎯 Business Recommendations

1. Focus on High-Performing Markets

Continue focusing on the UK while exploring opportunities in other international markets.

2. Improve Seasonal Planning

Use monthly revenue trends to prepare inventory and marketing campaigns before high-demand periods.

3. Separate Product and Postage Revenue

Separate physical product sales from postage/service-related transactions for more accurate product performance analysis.
